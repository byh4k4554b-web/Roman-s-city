# Roman's City

A free, walkable 3D world in the browser, home of **El Vestuario** — an independent store reselling new, authentic sportswear (purchases happen on Depop or eBay).

Walk the street, browse the store, grab a virtual drink at Sydney's café or the Cuper Juice bar, take penalties at the Cuper Cup pitch, and ride the chairlift up Cuper Peak for a ski or snowboard run.

**Play:** https://byh4k4554b-web.github.io/Roman-s-city/

Everything runs in a single `index.html` (three.js, MIT License). Brand names belong to their owners; El Vestuario is not affiliated with them. See the in-game *Legal* notice for details.
